# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical recruiters, engineering leaders, and potential clients evaluating Gabriel Vilabracho’s work, technical judgment, and architectural experience.

## Product Purpose

Present selected projects and engineering case studies with enough clarity and evidence for visitors to understand the problem, the architectural decisions, and Gabriel’s contribution.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, editorial, and technical. The portfolio should communicate confidence through evidence, structure, and precise visual hierarchy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS layouts, decorative glassmorphism, neon AI aesthetics, excessive gradients, and interfaces that prioritize visual effects over technical clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make technical reasoning visible.
2. Prefer evidence over promotional claims.
3. Use typography and spacing to establish hierarchy.
4. Keep visual treatments intentional and restrained.
5. Preserve consistency across project and case-study pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA color contrast, semantic document structure, keyboard accessibility, visible focus states, readable line lengths, and reduced-motion support.
